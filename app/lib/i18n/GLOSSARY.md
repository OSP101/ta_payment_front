# English glossary — COCO TAS

Use these renderings everywhere so the same Thai term never comes out two ways.
Tone: plain, short UI English (sentence case for labels and buttons, no
trailing period on labels). Address the reader as "you". Keep numbers, codes,
course codes, section numbers and academic-year labels (2569/1) as they are.
